# Background Music — drop your own royalty-free tracks here

ClipForge **does not ship with any music** (to avoid shipping copyrighted
audio). Add your own royalty-free `.mp3` / `.m4a` / `.wav` files into the
category folders below. They'll show up automatically in the music picker.

```
public/music/
├── energetic/
├── chill/
├── dramatic/
└── funny/
```

## Where to get free, license-safe music

- **YouTube Audio Library** — https://studio.youtube.com → Audio Library
  (filter by "No attribution required").
- **Pixabay Music** — https://pixabay.com/music/ (free for commercial use).
- **Free Music Archive** — https://freemusicarchive.org (check each license).

> ⚠️ Do **not** drop copyrighted/commercial songs here. Your clips can get
> muted or struck on TikTok/Shorts. Stick to royalty-free / CC0 tracks.

Files in these folders are git-ignored on purpose so you never commit audio
you don't own.
